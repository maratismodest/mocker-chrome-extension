# Privacy Policy for Mock Data Extension

Last updated: 2026-10-12

## What Data We Collect

Mock Data Extension does not collect any personal data, browsing history or website content.

The only data the extension keeps is what you enter yourself:

- the mock endpoints you create (HTTP method, URL path and the JSON response to return);
- whether mocking is turned on or off;
- the unsaved contents of the popup form (method, endpoint and response), so they are not lost when the popup closes.

## How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally in your browser on your device. Nothing is synced to your
Google account or sent to any server.

## How Data Is Used

Your mock endpoints are used only to return the response you defined when a page you
visit requests a matching URL path. The extension does not read, record or send the
requests themselves or the pages you visit.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics or tracking.

## Data Sharing

Your data is not shared with anyone and never leaves your device. If you use the
Export button, the extension saves your mock endpoints to a JSON file on your computer;
what you do with that file is up to you.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. You can remove a single endpoint with "Remove Item",
delete everything with "Clear All", or uninstall the extension, which removes all of its data.

## Changes to This Policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the
"Last updated" date above will change. Significant changes will be noted in the
extension's release notes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at
